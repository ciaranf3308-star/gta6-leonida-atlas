GTADB / Map GTA

GTA VI Landmarks Data and GTA VI Map Tiles are provided by GTADB / Map GTA and its contributors. The 2D basemap is GTADB's In-Game Day map tiles (map artwork © Rockstar Games); the 3D explorer uses the Yanis v16-derived raster, distributed through GTADB.

The pinned revision is 2e9e194b497d7a1a7da4f2288c1065dc0b913e80. The catalogue contains 4,380 records: 4,286 positioned records and 94 without coordinates.

Pinned map source · Pinned landmark source · Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International.

Our presentation recolors and generalizes source pixels, excludes non-geographic legend and screenshot areas, and adds decorative coast emphasis. The water halo is not bathymetry. No source-author endorsement is implied.

gta6-leonida-map-data / gta6maps.io

County stunt-jump counts (130 across Leonida) and the 100% completion thresholds (19 categories, 1,325 required) come from gta6-leonida-map-data (gta6maps.io), pinned at commit 1c5977b. The dataset is licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International and archived at Zenodo DOI 10.5281/zenodo.24412576.

Only the aggregate counts were used: stunt jumps per county and the per-category required / total numbers. The dataset's 798 marker positions were NOT imported — they are schematic coordinates on the source's own map, typically ~2 km from the verified GTADB position for the same place, and every one of the 774 markers that match a GTADB record duplicates a place the atlas already carries. County extents shown in the atlas remain estimates: the official poster prints county names but no boundary lines.

Visual direction and reconstruction

An illustrated map informed the original hierarchy, restrained linework and broad color language only; its coastline, labels and geometry were not reused. Procedural geometry and generated material or vegetation studies are reconstruction assets, not extracted GTA VI game assets. Their rights status remains separate from the CC BY 4.0 map data.

Built with

React and TypeScript provide the application, Vite builds static files, Leaflet provides the main map interactions, Dexie handles IndexedDB storage, and Three.js renders the optional reconstruction. The travel map retains its native SVG coordinate layer. Interface typography uses Inter and Oswald. Dependencies retain their own licenses.
